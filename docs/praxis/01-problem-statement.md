| Industry | Aerospace / Aviation |
| Elaboration 1 | The problem affects aerospace maintenance personnel, who require real-time decision-making to maintain safe flight and reliable lifespan of aircraft engine components. |
| Elaboration 2 | Unaddressed degradation of aircraft engine components leads to catastrophic failures, severe flight schedule disruptions, and constraints on on-ground aircraft support resources. |
